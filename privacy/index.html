Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

The short version: SoapWiz has no account, collects nothing about you, and sends nothing to us.

What we collect

Nothing. SoapWiz has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app contains no networking code that sends your data anywhere.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — ingredients, purchases, recipes, batches, photos, and settings — is stored on your device. If you use iCloud, this data syncs across your own devices through your private iCloud database. That database belongs to your Apple account; the developer cannot see or access it.

Camera and photos

SoapWiz uses the camera only when you ask it to — to photograph your soap or to scan a printed recipe so it can be imported. Photos you add are stored with your app data, on your device and in your private iCloud. They are never uploaded to us.

Recipe import

When you import a recipe from text or a photo, the analysis runs entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence (Apple's on-device Foundation Models). The recipe text and images are not sent to any server.

Notifications

Any notifications, such as ingredient expiry reminders, are scheduled locally on your device. They involve no server and no external service.

Exports and sharing

Backups and shared recipe files (.soapwizrecipe) are created only when you choose to export or share them. They go wherever you send them — the developer receives nothing.

Children

SoapWiz does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

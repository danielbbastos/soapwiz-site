Frequently asked questions

How is my data stored?

On your device, and — if you turn on iCloud — synced across your own devices through your private iCloud database. See the Privacy Policy.

Do you collect any data about me?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.

How do I import a recipe?

In the Recipes tab, choose import, then paste recipe text or scan or select a photo. The import is analysed on your device using Apple Intelligence, so nothing is sent to a server. Recipe import needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence.

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings → Export Data saves everything to a single file you can store or transfer. Import Data restores from such a file, replacing what is currently in the app.

How do I share a recipe?

Share a recipe as a .soapwizrecipe file. The recipient can open it in SoapWiz from Mail, Messages, or Files.

A note on lye safety

Soap making uses caustic lye. SoapWiz's calculations are a helper, not a guarantee — always double-check with a second source and follow proper safety precautions.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email soapwiz@icloud.com with as much detail as you can.
